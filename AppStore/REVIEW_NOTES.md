# App Review notes — paste into App Store Connect › App Review Information › Notes

RodentLimb is a research video-analysis utility for laboratory mouse behavior. No sign-in or account is required, and the app makes no network connections (the help links in Settings open the online manual in Safari).

How to review:
1. Launch the app. A three-page welcome appears on first launch; tap Continue, then Get started.
2. Tap "New analysis", choose "Tail suspension", tap "Add videos" › "From Files" (or Photos), and select the demo video linked below.
3. Tap "Analyze". On-device pose detection runs (about real-time on recent iPhones). Keep the app in the foreground.
4. The result opens with three tabs: Summary (key numbers and chart), Review (video with keypoints) and Measures (all values).
5. Tap "Parameters" and move a slider; results update immediately. Tap "Export" to share the CSV files with the system share sheet.
