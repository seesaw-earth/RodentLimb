## Description (≤ 4000)

RodentLimb analyzes mouse behavior videos at the level of the limbs, entirely on your iPhone or iPad. A pose model finds the four paws, the tail base, the nose and the mouth in every frame. The app then scores three widely used motor and behavior tests.

TAIL SUSPENSION
• Immobility time and %, over minutes 2–6 by default
• Latency, number and duration of immobility bouts, per-minute profile
• Tail-climbing flag and an estimated hindlimb clasping score

CYLINDER TEST
• Left, right and both-paw wall contacts during rearing
• Contralateral use and asymmetry index relative to the lesioned hemisphere
• Replay every contact and correct its label; your edits are kept

GAIT ANALYSIS
• Stance, swing, stride length and frequency, duty factor
• Base of support, step length, print position
• Interlimb phase, coupling strength, regularity index, gait pattern (walk, trot, gallop, bound)
• Nose and tail-base lateral sway
• Speed-matched values at a common reference speed, plus straight-walking and run-end filters

BUILT FOR RESEARCH WORKFLOWS
• Import one or many videos from Photos or Files; they are processed in a background queue
• The model runs once per video; thresholds, windows and calibration can be changed afterwards and results update instantly
• Review player with keypoints drawn on the video, frame-by-frame scrubbing and playback speeds
• Measures in body lengths, so settings carry over between cameras; optional cm calibration
• Group comparison with every animal shown
• English CSV exports and a methods paragraph listing your parameters and references

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no network connection, no analytics. Videos and results stay on your device until you share them. The source code is public, and a full user manual is online in English and Chinese.

Interface languages: English and Simplified Chinese.

Research use: automated scores support, but do not replace, blinded review of the video. Validate each setup against manual scoring.
